The first photograph has a deep depth of field and by using the camera raw filter I was able to adjust the hue/saturation in photoshop and durastically alter the color of the original photograph to a more blue/cyan filter. It created a nice effect that reminded me of the blue filter used in twilight. I also used the straighten tool which allowed me to adjust symmetry of the photograph. In addition to those effects I made sure to use different layers to change the intensity of the saturation and contrast. For the second photograph I used the same tools on photoshop but I opted for a preset filter in addition to the white balance tool that made the photograph warmer, slightly sepia.
