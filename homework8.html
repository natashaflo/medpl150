Homework 8:Animated GIF's

This project taught me the most as I struggled with this the most but it taught me multiple tools and how to acheive what I saw in my head. I utilized both photoshop and illustrator to create the layers as a stack in illustrator and then loaded them into photoshop and used the timeline tool to create the movement that I wanted them to follow. The eye gif used the extrude and bevel tool on photoshop to acheive the sort of 90's style that was created. The puppet warp tool I used for the kermit gif used a png of kermit and then created a series of layers and edited them in the timeline tool to create the dancing. The third gif was a combination of a photograph and illustrations I created on illustrator and later followed the same process as the first gif.
