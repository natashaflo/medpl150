Homework 4: Photomontage

Collage 1: Immigration

The Bracero program was an agreement between the US and Mexico that exploited Mexican workers in exchange for temporary migration to the U.S. My collage attempts to depict the ongoing issue with immigration and racism in the United States and how people of color have been historically exploited by the American government for its benefit. I used an image from the Bracero program for the foreground because I wanted them to be depicted as the focal point of the image. The second layer is the image of the white house which i wanted to add some contrast to by bringing up the saturation and hue to the flowers and shrubs at the center to create a partition between the bars of the border and the foreground of the workers. I used several masks for the image overall and I used different types of the object selection tools because each image required some adjustment. I used the file place embedded tool to place the constitution in the layer underneath to depict the foundation of American values and how equal opportunity is embedded into our foundation. I also utilized different blending tools such as the drop shadow effect to create more definition and contrast from certain subjects and the background.

Collage 2: Animal Entertainment

The concept for this collage was to depict the abuse animals endure through being forced to perform as entertainment for audiences. I used three different textures to depict this specifically using the red ink texture as the most eye catching element of the image. I also kept it the only other layer that is in color apart from the bear layer because I wanted these two subjects to stand out the most and to highlight the abuse these animals endure for the entertainment of people. The color scheme is very muted in that I used grays and blacks for the other layers because I wanted to show how something that is often associated with bright colors and loud patterns can hide the dull reality behind these performances that people look away from. I used various blending modes for the tent, bear and ball layers because I wanted to have them definition that would separate them from the background layers and highlight how they are meant to be the subject that yours eyes are immediately drawn to. The bevel and emboss mode applied a 3D effect to the tent layer which provided more depth and highlighted certain details of the tent itself. I used various masks for each image by using the object selection tools and the select subject tool that took longer to process but was more much more accurate for smaller details like the fur on the bear.
