Data Visualization

For this project I wanted to depict the relationship between phone use and the amount of hours slept as a result. I conducted my poll on my social media and posed the questions: 1. How many hours do you sleep a day? Rate 1-10. 2. How many hours of your day are spent on your phone? Rank 1-10, 3. Rank your energy levels 1-10. The first graph depicts how the 10 people in my poll ranked the amount of time slept. for instance, the x-axis is the amount of hours slept 1-10, while the y-axis is the participants 1-10. I wanted to keep the graphs very minimal in terms of design because the illustrator tool was quite challenging and understanding the data entry tool was quite challenging. The second graph follows the same format in that it is a regular bar graph with the x-axis being the participants 1-10. The y-axis measures the amount of hours spent on the phone 1-10. It is similar to the first graph in that it is also minimalistic in regards to the color palette, shape, placement, and font type. The third pie chart graph follows a similar color palette to the previous two. I decided to separate the lowest percentage from the rest of the percentages. The third graph depicts their rankings in percentages, with the minority being the people who ranked their energy levels lowest the same people who ranked their phone usage hours on the higher end.
