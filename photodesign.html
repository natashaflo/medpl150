Photomontage and Design

I decided to use the second collage because I felt that it could benefit the most from adding a design, as it is less visually noisy than the other collage. I created the eye in illustrator then opted for an iris color that would stand out from the rest of the colors in the collage without drawing attention away from the main subject and focal point of the collage. I wanted to add the red tear drop to symbolize the blood shed by circus animals. I also wanted to create some cohesion between the red tear drop and the blood texture layer underneath.
